Why We Ventilate

🫁

Safety

Maintain O₂ ≥ 20.5% for safe entry. Remove poisonous, flammable or fumigant gases from cargo holds.

Check O₂ before entry

Cargo Care

Prevent cargo sweat and ship sweat. Both cause rust, mould and multi-million dollar claims.

Use dew point rule

Three Decision Rules

Hot → Cold
Ventilate Boldly

Cargo warm, outside cold. Hold air more humid than outside — bring outside air in.

Cold → Hot
Do NOT Ventilate

Outside air warm and humid. Opening holds introduces moisture onto cold cargo — cargo sweat guaranteed.

No Hygrometer
3-Degree Rule

Ventilate only if outside temp is ≥ 3°C LOWER than cargo temp. Hygroscopic cargo only.

⑤ Understanding DP Delta

DP Delta = Hold Dew Point − Outside Dew Point
+5.6°C
VENTILATE
Hold MORE humid than outside. Outside air drier — it removes moisture. Larger positive = safer to ventilate.
−3.2°C
NO VENT
Outside MORE humid. Bringing it in ADDS moisture, causes cargo sweat. Seal all vents immediately.
≈ 0°C
⚠ MONITOR
Equal dew points. No benefit ventilating. Re-measure every 30 min. Stop for steel cargo (RH >60% = rust risk).
Example: Hold DP=14.2°C, Outside DP=7.6°C → Delta=+6.6°C → Ventilate confidently.
Steel warning: RH >60% = exponential rust even with positive delta. Use dehumidification.

How to Use VentCalc
1
Create a Voyage
Go to Log tab → tap New Voyage. Give it a name and vessel. Set it as active.
2
Enter Outside Air
In Calculator → Outside Air block. Measure dry & wet bulb at hold exhaust with a hygrometer. Dew point calculates automatically.
3
Fill Hold Readings
Enter cargo type, dry & wet bulb for each loaded hold. Scroll the table right. Decision shows instantly — green = ventilate, red = no vent.
4
Add Weather & Save
Fill in sea state and conditions in the Weather block. Tap Add to Voyage Log. Do this at least twice daily.
5
Export at Port
Log tab → Export → PDF or Excel. Attach to voyage file. This is your legal defence against cargo damage claims.

Welcome to VentCalc

The professional marine cargo ventilation guide for Chief Officers. Dew point calculations, voyage logs, trend charts — all offline.

The Calculator

Enter dry and wet bulb temperatures for each hold. The app calculates dew point, relative humidity, and instantly tells you: Ventilate or No Vent.

The Golden Rule

Ventilate ONLY when the dew point of outside air is LOWER than hold dew point. DP Delta shows you the margin at a glance.

Your Legal Defence

The Voyage Log is your legal defence against cargo claims. Create voyages, add entries twice daily, export as PDF or Excel for your voyage file.
