THE RULE — Ventilate only when outside dew point is lower than or equal to hold dew point.

Why we ventilate
Safety

Maintain O₂ ≥ 20.5% for safe entry. Remove poisonous, flammable or fumigant gases from cargo holds.

Check O2 first
Cargo care

Prevent cargo sweat and ship sweat. Both cause rust, mould and multi-million dollar claims.

Use the dew point rule
Three decision rules
Hot to cold
VENTILATE BOLDLY

Cargo warm, outside cold. Hold air more humid than outside — bring outside air in.

Cold to hot
DO NOT VENTILATE

Outside air warm and humid. Opening holds introduces moisture onto cold cargo — cargo sweat guaranteed.

No hygrometer
3-DEGREE RULE

Ventilate only if outside temp is ≥3°C lower than cargo temp. Hygroscopic cargo only.

Understanding DP Delta

DELTA = HOLD DEW POINT − OUTSIDE DEW POINT
+5.6 C
VENTILATE
Hold more humid than outside. Outside air drier — it removes moisture. Larger positive = safer.
−3.2 C
NO VENT
Outside more humid. Bringing it in adds moisture, causes cargo sweat. Seal all vents.
≈ 0.0 C
MONITOR
Equal dew points. No benefit ventilating. Re-measure in 30 min. Stop for steel if RH>60%.

How to use VentCalc
  • 1
    Create a voyage. Log tab — tap New Voyage. Name it, set vessel, set active.
  • 2
    Enter outside air. Calculator — Outside Air block. Measure dry and wet bulb at hold exhaust.

    Fill hold readings. Enter cargo type, dry and wet bulb per hold. Decision shows instantly.

    Add weather and save. Fill sea state and conditions. Tap Add to Voyage Log. Twice daily minimum.

    Export at port. Log tab — Export — PDF or Excel. Attach to voyage file as legal defence.
